Gir Wildlife Coexistence Guidelines / વન્યજીવ સુરક્ષા નિયમો
Simple, essential safety protocols for living peacefully alongside lions and leopards.
🦁 Facing an Asiatic Lion (સિંહ સામે આવે ત્યારે):
Apex Carnivore
  • DO NOT RUN (ક્યારેય ભાગવું નહીં): Running triggers predatory chase instinct. Stand ground calmly.
  • Maintain eye contact: Back away slowly step-by-step without turning your back.
  • Speak loudly or clap: Lions naturally avoid confident human sounds.
  • Always carry lights: Carry a high-beam torch and bamboo stick (*લાઠી*) after sunset.
🐆 Facing a Leopard (દીપડો સામે આવે ત્યારે):
Stealth Predator
  • Appear larger: Raise arms or hold your cloth/turban high above head.
  • Keep children close: Never allow children or small animals to roam outside at dawn or dusk.
  • Avoid dense sugarcane crops: Leopards frequently shelter in sugarcane (*શેરડી*) fields.
  • Cover livestock pens: Ensure wire netting over the top of pens to prevent leopards jumping inside.
